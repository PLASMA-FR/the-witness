LOCAL-FIRST · OPEN SOURCE · AUDITABLE

Don’t let the first AI answer be the final answer.

The Witness sits between an AI app and its model endpoint. It checks each candidate response with a local Gemma judge, blocks weak output, repairs the prompt, retries, and releases only an approved response.

WHAT CHANGES

A trust layer, not another chatbot.

The user’s request stays the same. The Witness adds a private verification loop around the upstream model response.

1

Intercept

The local proxy forwards the request, then holds the candidate response before the app sees it.

2

Judge

A Gemma 4 judge returns structured scores, confidence, risk, and one of three verdicts.

3

Repair

Rejected output becomes a hidden retry instruction. The original user request is not rewritten.

4

Record

Verdicts, repairs, retries, endpoint errors, and human decisions go into a JSONL audit trail.

NO MAGIC CLAIMS

A firewall reduces risk. It does not guarantee truth.

The judge model can still be wrong. Local performance depends on hardware, model choice, and backend. High-risk medical, legal, financial, destructive coding, emergency, and safety-critical workflows still need qualified human review.

Current MVP support is focused on non-streaming OpenAI-compatible chat completions.
